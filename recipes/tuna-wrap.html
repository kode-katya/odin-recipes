Description

A tuna wrap is a delightful and satisfying meal made with a soft tortilla that envelopes a flavorful mixture of ingredients. The centerpiece is flaky, tender tuna, often mixed with creamy mayonnaise for richness. It's typically combined with crunchy vegetables like crispy lettuce, juicy tomatoes, and thinly sliced cucumbers freshness. Optional additions include shredded carrots, red onion, or sweet corn for extra texture and flavor. Some recipes also include tangy elements like pickles or a hint of lemon juice, as well as seasonings such as salt, pepper, and a dash of paprika or dill. The wrap is rolled tightly to create a portable, wholesome meal perfect for a quick lunch or on-the-go snack.

Instructions

  1. Get a bowl.
  2. Get your canned tuna & open it. How? You'll have to find a video.
  3. Put your canned tuna (after opening it) into the bowl.
  4. Get your avocado, cut it in half & scoop half of it into the bowl. If you got the budget, you can also use the other half, but I only got the budget to use half of it.
  5. smash the avocado with a fork and mix it in with the tuna.
  6. Wash your celery, tomatoes (for some reason the plural version of tomato looks incorrect) & cucmbers. How much should you use? I don't know; you decide your own future.
  7. Cut everyhting up using a knife and a cutting board (cutting board goes under). I like to cut it up based off of how I'm feeling. If you're feeling square, cut them up into squares. If you want triangles, do triangles. Cut them up however you want. I won't judge.
    • Tomatoes - I cut them in half. Cutting them into fourths is too much work.
    • Cucumbers- lightsaber sticks
    • Celery - thin suhi roles
  8. Add your vegetables to the bowl & mix them slightly.
  9. If you didn't so already, get your totilla wrap out & put your tuna mixture onto the wrap.
  10. Try to wrap your tortilla.
